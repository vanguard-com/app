# Lumi support

Email [projectvanguard.sg@gmail.com](mailto:projectvanguard.sg@gmail.com) for help, accessibility
issues or privacy questions. Include your phone model, OS version, Lumi version, what you tried
and what happened. Do not include a private video unless you intend to share it with support.

Lumi needs iOS 16.4 or later, or Android 10 or later on a 64-bit ARM phone. Camera, microphone
and volume-key behavior require an installed native build; Expo Go cannot run Lumi.

## Common questions

- **Camera or microphone is off:** open Subject > Settings > More > App access. Select the
  access type and Allow, or Open phone settings. Videos without microphone access are silent.
- **No directional tone while filming:** recording cues use earphones by default. Connect
  earphones, or review the recording cue preference. Check system volume and Cue preferences.
- **Volume keys do not record on iPhone:** this needs iOS 17.2 or later and an active camera.
  VoiceOver shortcuts may own the keys. The on-screen Record action remains available.
- **How to save/share:** Review offers Save (Photos) and Share (another installed app).
  Sharing works with the video in Lumi even when Photos access is off. Finish posting in the
  app you choose. Retake removes Lumi's copy; any Photos copy remains.
- **Scene descriptions are unavailable:** the bundled object list works without model downloads.
  Models need free space, enough RAM and an explicit download. Standard needs about 546 MB;
  Detailed about 3.91 GB. Descriptions may be inaccurate.
- **Learn the controls again:** Subject > Settings > More > Replay tutorial. Practice saves
  no recording. App access and your existing settings are preserved.
- **Recorded voice measurements are unavailable:** enable Voice feedback and record with sound.
  Pause playback before analysis. Pace additionally requires supported on-device recognition.

Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for SDK diagnostics, live command recognition, model
downloads, storage, backups and sharing.
