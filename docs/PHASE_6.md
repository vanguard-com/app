# Phase 6: review, playback, sharing and voice commands

Branch: `phase-6-review-sharing`, from Phase 5 merge `46f3839`. Phase 5's final Android APK and unsigned iOS device builds passed. Phase 6 requires a new native build for `expo-video` and `expo-sharing`; physical-phone acceptance is still required. Keep the phase PR unmerged until the owner confirms the checks below.

## Behavior

Review displays and speaks the video length, recorded-audio status, measured framing/lighting coverage, and any enabled feedback/recorded-voice results. It offers exactly four large actions: **Play, Retake, Save, Share**. Summary statistics continue to persist for the latest 20 recordings in MMKV. Retake removes Lumi's video and its summary; it preserves a saved Photos copy. Lumi retains its local video after saving/sharing so it can be played or shared again.

Play opens a separate playback screen and starts the original local video, including its audio. Accessible Pause/Play and From beginning controls replace the native video controls. Playback stops on background, navigation, end or decoder error; returning to the app leaves it paused. Commands are suspended throughout playback, including initial buffering and native buffering/interruption transitions, so words inside the recording cannot cause a save/share/retake. Pause remains available during buffering/interruption and explicitly allows commands again. Background playback, external playback, picture-in-picture and media notifications are disabled. No autoplay resumes on returning from another app.

Optional **Voice analysis** now lives on playback: pause, then choose it, or say “analyse my voice” from Review to go there directly. It requires the existing Live feedback master and Voice preference, and a recording with sound. Analysis and playback cannot run together. File recognition keeps its existing exclusive lease until native shutdown, including cancellation; recorded speech never becomes a command. The accessible Cancel analysis button remains usable while command listening is suspended.

Share, or Review's “post video” command, exports the file already saved in Lumi's documents folder through the OS share sheet. Capture moves it there before attempting Photos and before showing Review. Photos denial/failure therefore does not prevent playback or sharing, and Share does not ask for Photos permission again. Save retries Photos explicitly and does not make another copy after success. Video MIME/UTI matches MP4 or MOV. Missing/empty files, unavailable sharing and native failures receive useful spoken errors. Save/share cannot start twice, and other Review actions are disabled while either owns the file. A pending availability lookup cannot open a sheet after Review loses focus.

The installed receiving apps determine which targets appear: select YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp or another compatible app when offered, then finish the receiving app's posting flow. Cancelling preserves the video. Lumi cannot tell whether the target posted anything and never announces upload success. iPad uses the library's native popover handling. No direct upload, OAuth, account or API key is added. YouTube Data API upload remains a v2 option (D44).

## Measurements and limits

- Framing samples at the existing face cadence in Framing mode. “Fully in frame” means the selected face's positive, finite preview bounds are entirely inside the visible image; a detected-but-cropped or absent face counts as out of frame. It is face framing only (D31), not full body or object tracking.
- Face lighting uses the existing same-frame luminance analysis: 1 Hz in Framing/Background, 2 Hz in Lighting. It does not add another CameraX analysis output. A valid face measurement is required; a face that is missing or too small is unmeasured.
- Feedback mode keeps its Phase 4 head-facing/expression/posture statistics; framing and lighting are explicitly unmeasured there. Lighting mode does not invent framing data. No raw frames, boxes, luminance grids or transcripts are persisted.
- Percentages are time weighted and include the **measured duration**, rather than claiming coverage of the whole clip. Gaps over one second (framing) or 1.5 seconds (lighting) are excluded. A continuously measured dark tail is reported only if at least one second; unavailable/stalled data clears the tail. These are estimates pending phone calibration.
- Duration ends when Stop is requested rather than including file finalization/Photos permission time. A native completion without an explicit Stop uses its completion time. It is a session estimate, not container metadata.

Voice help now describes the current screen's commands. Camera retains framing/lighting/background/feedback commands and its default of no command microphone during recording; Settings can opt into listening while recording. Review supports play, save, retake, post, analysis and help. Playback listens while paused; use Pause or Cancel analysis buttons during media operations. Unsupported contexts explain which screen to use. Late results after abort and asynchronous capability lookup after navigation are ignored.

## Phone acceptance

Run on both Android and iPhone, with screen reader off and on, and record phone/OS/build/settings/results in TESTING.md. Also check iPad sharing and large text. Native compilation alone does not verify these behaviors.

1. Set the subject to Face, frame it, record speech and stop with the button/volume key. Expect Review with the four actions and a spoken length. Play must show the same original video and audible recording; no second capture, missing audio or rotated/stretched preview.
2. Record a face entirely visible, partly cropped, then absent. In Framing mode, check the measured durations and approximate percentages. Change to Lighting and record good/dark lighting ending with several dark seconds. Expect lighting coverage/dark tail and framing unmeasured. With no detectable face, unmeasured lighting must replace a reassuring percentage. Check Feedback mode with its toggles both on and off.
3. Pause, continue, restart, reach the end, leave playback with Back/hardware Back and background the app while playing. Playback must stop and remain paused on return. Check portrait/landscape, MP4/MOV, silent clips, earphones/Bluetooth and sound with the phone's silent switch. A decoder error must leave Review reachable for saving/sharing.
4. Put “retake video”, “post video”, “start recording” and “save video” inside the recording. Play and analyse it: no command may fire. While paused, speak a normal command and verify listening resumes. Deny microphone/speech permission: every core action must still work with buttons.
5. Turn on Live feedback and Voice, pause and run Voice analysis. Expect progress/Cancel, the existing local-only voice metrics and preserved video audio. Play/Restart stay disabled during analysis. Cancel or leave during recognition, return and retry; no simultaneous playback/recognition or stale result may attach to a new video.
6. Fresh install: accept, deny and permanently deny Photos add access in separate runs. Capture must remain in Lumi on denial, with Save retry instructions. Save after granting permission must add one usable video to Photos; repeated Save must not duplicate it. Play/Share must work after denial without another Photos request.
7. From Review choose Share and repeat with “post video”. Expect one system sheet for the locally saved video. Choose YouTube if installed/offered, complete its compose/upload flow, and check playable video/audio at the destination. Repeat with available TikTok/Instagram/WhatsApp targets. Record absent targets/platform restrictions; Lumi does not guarantee that every app registers a share extension.
8. Cancel the sheet, cancel a target's follow-up dialog, return from a receiving app, then share again. The video and summary must remain. Double tap Share or repeat the spoken command: only one sheet. Retake/Save/Play must be disabled while the sheet is pending. Test on iPad for a properly presented sheet without a popover crash.
9. Save to Photos, then Retake. Expect Camera, the app copy/summary removed, and the Photos copy preserved. Retake an unsaved video: only that app copy is removed. Sharing must not silently delete the original.
10. With VoiceOver/TalkBack and the largest text, reach every action, hear labels/hints and read the summary. Review has four controls, playback at most three plus Back, and all app controls are at least 60 points. Ask for help on each screen; only applicable commands should be listed. Verify ordinary Camera commands and the default microphone pause during recording still work.

Cloud tests cannot complete the YouTube handoff, OS share UI, real video decode, audio routing, screen reader or camera accuracy checks. Those acceptance results must come from phones.
